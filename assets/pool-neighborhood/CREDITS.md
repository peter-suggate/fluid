# Poolside guests

Innerscene authored the poses, garments and props using MakeHuman/MPFB community character and skin assets. Both source models are published under CC0 1.0. The pool derivatives close and resample the dressed silhouettes, transfer their original colour maps and remove the host’s parcel.

- [Taylor, placing an item ahead](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/placing-an-item-ahead-3d-person-everyday-tasks-3904157a)
- [Nora, relaxing on a sofa](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/woman-relaxing-on-a-sofa-3d-person-with-arm-along-the-back-5a6a84cc)

The daylight hemisphere comes from Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest’s CC0 [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky). The Blender sky bake samples the upper hemisphere, limits the solar disc and packs scene-linear colour for the interactive renderer.
