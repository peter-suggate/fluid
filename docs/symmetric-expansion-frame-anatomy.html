The subject

One centred water block collapsing across the smallest dyadic tank

A 1.6 × 0.8 × 1.6 m closed tank with free-slip walls holding one centred 0.8 × 0.4 × 0.8 m body (fill fraction ⅛), seeded as four bricks. It is the smallest dyadic domain that leaves an equal one-brick gap to all four walls, which is why it is the project's D4 symmetry oracle: volume, velocity, pressure, topology and four-wall contact must stay exactly symmetric under the dihedral group of the horizontal plane.

The two descriptor counts are §6.1 measured in the wild

The paper defines exactly two topology cases — cells with no coarser neighbour (an 18-bit neighbour mask, 218 possibilities) and cells with no finer neighbour (3 bits of child position plus 6 bits of coarse-neighbour direction, 29 possibilities), plus one discriminating bit.

The catalog those descriptors index is generated offline rather than populated on demand as the paper does: , max face incidence 30, max 36 neighbour rows, worst float32 geometry error 5.7 × 10⁻⁸. The paper reserves ~128 MB for its on-demand table; ours is smaller because it stores only the configurations the grading rule can actually produce.

Where the machine goes

The whole-device counters, and the four shapes that explain them

These are frame-wide totals. The per-band verdicts, the interactive mechanisms and the stability series all live in the eight bands — this section only names the shapes and says where each one is.

The device is busy 96 % of the time and using about 12 % of its threads and 15 % of its ALUs to do it. Aggregate memory traffic is 4.3 GB/s — on a part whose observed streaming peak in this same profiler is ~78 GB/s, that is 5.5 % of achievable bandwidth.

For scale: the paper reports (Table 1, Intel Xeon E3-1241) a streaming-copy reference of 14.45 GB/s and their optimised second-order Laplacian sustaining 3.95 GB/s. Our whole frame — on a GPU with an order of magnitude more bandwidth — moves data at roughly the rate their single-socket CPU kernel did. The bottleneck is not arithmetic intensity; it is that the work is decomposed into launches too small and too serial to fill the machine.

Conclusions

The physics is not the cost

Every operator the paper spends its Section 4 and Section 6 on — the power-diagram Laplacian, the divergence, the gradient, the catalog lookup, the boundary coefficients — costs a combined of the frame. Section 6.1's central bet — a compact topology descriptor plus a lookup table instead of explicit meshing — pays off completely.

Third, and the lesson of this round: the wins came from deleting work, not from adding threads. Structured advection kept its 7.9 % residency ceiling and its 64-lane workgroups and still fell 10.6× — because three nested searches became indexed reads, and because the one genuinely divergent branch was hoisted into its own uniform pass. The fine level-set page halved by storing φ at the precision the band actually needs and deleting cached neighbour pointers that a key can recompute. Neither change touched the discretization.
