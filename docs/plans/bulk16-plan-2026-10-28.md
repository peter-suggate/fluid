# BULK16 plan (2026-10-28)

Goal: deep, quiet water costs almost nothing per page in time and memory, so
vast realtime scenes become possible. Baseline: main at 3500c506.

Sources: `nbflip-16h-progress.md` §2 (design), §4 (reader audit, depth
ladder); `nbflip-handoff-2026-10-28.md` §3A-B. Groundwork already in main:
`uniform-bulk-pages.ts` / `-reference.ts` (slot store, claim/retire,
fatal-on-exhaustion) with `tests/uniform-bulk-pages.test.ts`, not wired.

## Why

- Today a whole-16h page still owns a 4h block and is visited by about 20
  per-block 4h passes: 25 µs/page/step (depth ladder, pipelined timing) and
  1665 KiB/page running, 14.9 MiB/pool block at construction.
- At 100k bulk pages: about 2.5 s/step and about 159 GiB. Out of reach.
- A pressure-only slice was measured at about 3 µs/page. Only the whole
  design pays: the page leaves every 4h sweep and gives its block back.

## Targets (measured, pipelined timing, repeat-gated)

| metric | today | target |
|---|---|---|
| step cost per whole-16h page | 25 µs | ≤ 4 µs |
| memory per whole-16h page | 1665 KiB (+ NB-FLIP per-block buffers) | ≤ 4 KiB |
| closed rest drift at 1x-2.5x depth | 0 to −9e-5 | 0 |
| seep / letters (cross-scene table) | right tank fills every interval, \|drift\| ≤ 3%; letters ≤ 3% at 240 | unchanged or better |

## Design (from §2, kept minimal)

- A whole-16h page (all 8 regions bulk members, error test plus hysteresis
  pass) **demotes**: its block returns to the pool, and it takes a slot in the
  fixed 16h store. The page word becomes `BULK16 | slot`.
- Slot state: 8 pressures, region face-mean velocities, seam heads (the
  existing bulk record), an open/solid mask. 2-4 KiB.
- Every lattice predicate answers "liquid, no h, no surface, no particles"
  for BULK16. `umPageKind` keeps reading it SOLID-like, plus `umPageBulk`.
  The seven choke points and about 25 neighbour sites are in the §4 audit.
- **Pressure:** 8 rows at arena level 4 with the existing analytic coarse
  operator. The page leaves the level-2 lists.
- **Velocity:** `umUnitSample` falls back to a trilinear 16h face-mean
  sample. The page's own face means advect at 16h (one semi-Lagrangian
  trace per face mean).
- **Promote** (16h → 4h): claim a block, prolong face means linearly
  (`umBulkRow`), pressures piecewise plus hydrostatic. This conserves face
  flux exactly.
- **Demote** (4h → 16h): area-weighted face means, volume-weighted pressure.
  Conservative by construction.
- **Capacity:** both the block pool and the slot store are fixed at
  construction. A promote with no free block is an explicit fatal, never a
  silent refusal.
- Same rules as everywhere: no clamps or volume correction, fixed dt 1/60,
  no substeps, allocate upfront, no sim-time compilation.

### M1: page kind and readers, nothing demoted (gate passed)

**Rule.** A BULK16 word (`0x80000000|slot`) is a distinct kind,
`UM_BULK16=3` (host `UNIFORM_KIND.bulk`), and every reader recognises it
whatever its module binds. There are two classes of site:
- **Storage, held and wall-slot sites** answer "nothing 4h is stored across
  it", so they use a range test (`kind>=UM_SOLID`, or a word
  `>=0x80000000`). SOLID and BULK16 both have the top bit; block indices
  never do. The new constants are `UM_HELD_WORD` and `BN_HELD`, with the host
  twin `uniformHeldWord`.
- **Geometry sites** keep exact SOLID tests, because a BULK16 page is
  liquid inside the box, and the box fold says the same. These are
  `umPointHeld/Bounds`, `umSpanHeld`, `umVertexIncidentCell`, `umWallBlock`,
  `umSolidValid`, and solid-refinement 58.

### M2 step 2: test-only cycle harness on the tank (gate below)

`frame.bulkTransfer.cycle=true` is a test-only flag, not a runtime
setting. One commit demotes every resident block whose eight regions are
bulk members; the next promotes them all.

What a promoted block needs, with its source:
- **Phi:** the slot stores the 9^3 vertex block (skirt included) as it
  stood, since deep phi is a signed distance. That is 2.85 KiB of the
  slot. A coarser deep-phi image is a later memory option for vast scenes;
  it is not built.
- **Scratch bases:** the promote writes phi and faces into `phiScratch`
  and `velocityScratch` too. A deep vertex the advect holds keeps its
  scratch texel (`umHoldWarmDeep`), and forces read the forced faces
  before they are written.
- **Volume:** the surface volume counts 32^3 h cells per live slot
  (`uniform-mixed-surface-volume.ts`, `svBulkVolume`).
- **Census seed records** (`btSeedPromoted`, which runs after the pool
  commit and before pageMark, so census coupling stays out of pageMark):
  - stage 0 is the region's static authored fine tiles;
  - stages 1 and 2 (staged), or stage 5 (unstaged), are all ones;
  - the radius is ceil(8/4)+margin, a conservative bound from the
    membership reach (`UNIFORM_BULK_REACH_CELLS`).
  - The census exposes its records via
    `UniformMixedDynamicClassifier.residencyRecords`
    (`uniform-mixed-dynamic.ts`).
- **The 4h owners' cell fields** (`btPromoteCells`, `btPromoteLiquid`):
  - centerPhi is the eight-corner mean;
  - the fill target is open when all corners are below −1e-20 (as
    `gcEvaluate` gives it);
  - volume, volumeScratch and phase are 1.
- **Claimed HOT:** the NB-FLIP init hook keeps promoted regions HOT, per
  the lead's ruling (d).

**(A) composite FAC plus velocity: BULK16 pages in the solve** (A1, the
lead's approval: the Galerkin-coupled interface at level 4, slot-indexed rows).
- A native 4h row across a BULK16 face is kind `N_BULKN`. Its coupling to
  the page is c = 25.6·V/h4² per subface (16 a face); its head across is the
  slot's region pressure plus q = start − head, saved in
  `rootArena[R_MINIMUM+s]` as for any seam.
- A BULK16 region's row is the sum of its 64 native-scaled rows
  (K = 256/h4²): the subfaces' Σc(q − P) + 64·R_RHS[s]; BULK–BULK faces
  −256ρ·sign·ū*/(h·dt); +4096ρ·g_z/dt (the authority's gravity).
- `pWrite` gives a wall slot facing a BULK16 page the page's pressure
  4h across (p + (across − p)/θ, θ 2.5 or 4).
- Projection: BULK–BULK faces u = u* − dt/ρ·ΔP/16h. A face toward a
  resident block takes the mean of its sixteen subfaces' projected flux
  (it used to copy u*, which gained −g·dt a step).
- Kernels in the arena's bulk module (`pBulkRows`, `pBulkProject`,
  `pBulkCommit`) and its tail levels (`pBulkBake`, `pBulkLift`); the
  composite operator and the existing tail channels are reused, with no new
  knob and no extra cycle.
- **Storage**: the 9 rows a slot holds on tail levels 3, 4 and 5 are
  slot-indexed in the slot's own record (`BULK16_PAGE.tail3/tail4/tail5`),
  not packed into resident blocks' tail words. The record is 960 words:
  page, flags, generation, open, promote, 8 pressures, 36 face means and
  their next, the next pressures and rhs, the 9³ phi block, then the tail
  rows. With the free/live/pending list words a slot costs 3852 B.
  The super-tail fatal (a slot row past level 5) is an explicit
  construction fatal, on the M5 list; lifting it adds rows at the record's
  end and moves nothing.

### Where it starts (measured on HEAD ad0a11f5)

- The h start (every scene's default but the seep's gate file) starts every
  liquid tile at h, so its t=0 need is every resident block: slots cannot
  help it. A coarse start (`fluid.tiers.start "4h"`) starts on the authored
  regions alone and lets the census refine; its h stores are compacted to
  slots after t=0 (`UniformDetailStorage.compact`, "auto").
- Under a coarse start the h tiles a build may ask for are capped by
  `fluid.tiers.bricks` (8 tiles a brick), a per-scene figure. A build over it
  is deferred and then fatal (`followMixedCapacity`). Letters under a coarse
  start at the seep's 512 bricks: "a build needs 4219 h tiles; fluid.tiers.bricks
  512 holds 4096" at step 1-2. The h slots themselves are claimed after the
  build (`UniformFineSlots.claim`), and an empty free stack is a fatal
  violation.
- The closed rest ladder under a coarse start is identical to today at 1x
  and 2.5x (drift 0, entry step 29, 36 and 108 members, the same peak

### What a refused tile does with its liquid

A refused tile is an ordinary 4h tile: the state the coarse start begins
in and every quiet band tile coarsens to. Nothing about it is new.
- Particles already live on 4h owners. They are the band's overlap
  (`nbSeedCell`: "the geometric particle band, including its overlap on 4h
  owners"), they deposit to the 4h faces (`transferOwners`, `transferSeams`),
  and their brick slabs are keyed by pool block (`ubBrickAt`), not by h slot.
  A particle in a refused tile keeps moving and depositing on the 4h grid. It
  is never deleted by a refusal; the deep and sparse retirement rules are
  unchanged.
- The liquid there is the grid's φ at 4h (Ferstl et al. 2016: outside the
  band the liquid is carried on the grid).
- An h load at a block with no slot reads the field's base (the sparse
  domain); a store there is dropped at slot 0. A tile is admitted at h only
  when every block its detail ring reaches holds a slot (below), so no h
  owner and no ring tile ever lands on slot 0.
- So priority 2 (particle closure) does not make refusal impossible: it
  orders who is served first. Its cost is accuracy, which the volume drift
  and the tile-steps at 4h report.

### The claim, in the layout build

One rule decides both the h tiles and their slots, before any tile word is
written:
1. Residents are never evicted: a claim takes only free slots, and a slot
   frees only through the quiet release (a block no admitted tile's ring
   reaches after the remap).
2. A tile the build asks for at h needs a slot for every block its ring
   reaches: the stored pages covering cells [4p − 13, 4p + 15] on each axis
   of tile p (the ring is the tiles within three of it, and a ring tile's
   corner vertices can sit in the high plane of the page below). That is at
   most two pages an axis, eight blocks. The slot mark (`fsRingLow`,
   `fsRingHigh` in `uniform-fine-slots.ts`) marks the same box for each h
   tile of the adopted generation, so the claim and the release agree.
3. Each tile the build asks for at h has a key: its level (particle closure
   5: a tile holding a live particle or within its travel plus the particle
   stage's margin of 2 tiles; static closure 4: the census's static bit for
   solid and body promotion and joins, or an authored fine region; band
   tiles by region heat, HOT 3, WARM 2, COLD 1) above its 16-cell region
   index. Every slotless block in a requesting tile's box takes the largest
   key of the tiles that need it. One workgroup bisects for the least key
   whose wanting blocks fit the free stack; every block at or above that cut
   pops a slot. Keys are per region, so every tile of a region at or above
   the cut is served whole and a region never takes half of what it needs.
   A region below the cut waits for the next build (no churn: what was
   served keeps its slots).
4. A tile any of whose blocks has no slot after the claim is refused: it
   stays 4h. The build counts refused tiles; the receipt carries the count,
   the claims, the free slots and their least (so the slots in use and
   their peak), and the info publishes them (`uniformMixedRefusedTiles`,
   `uniformMixedRefusedTileBuilds`, `uniformMixedSlotClaims`,
   `uniformMixedSlotCapacity`, `uniformMixedSlotsUsed`,
   `uniformMixedSlotPeak`).
This replaces the deferral (`admit`) and the bricks fatal under a slot
capacity, and the slot violation becomes unreachable. The h-tile ceiling
of the owner-indexed buffers is then 512 × slots (Bet B's ceiling), which
no admitted generation can pass.

### Step 2b: the particle store by rule, (A)

2. **Particle records by rule** (`uniform-particle-census.ts`): records =
   UNIFORM_FINE_SLOT_GROWTH (1.5) × 8 samples a band cell (Ferstl et al.

#### Coarse-start volume: the impact's step-1 loss (fixed)

Under `{start: "4h"}` the impact scene (a solid-walled basin, ball at rest
over it) lost 56–82% of its liquid in step 1, and none under the h start.

- Not BULK16, cold regions, the solid slots or the sparse domain alone.
- The loss is in the step-1 advance: phi's represented liquid drops in

#### O1 ruling: Sussman's band projection, and how it differs from U7 (2026-10-30)

The ruling is to project the extended velocity in the first two gas layers,
as in Sussman (2003, J. Comput. Phys. 187:110–136, the "extrapolated
velocity" projection; used in PARIS, Aniszewski et al. 2021, arXiv
2012.11744, "Ensuring volume conservation"). It sits behind the solver
option `airBand` (`"off"`, the default, or `"sussman"`) until it passes.

**What U7 was, and why it was removed.** Before the steer-back (3500c506;
`docs/plans/nbflip-steer-back-2026-10-28.md`, rows U6 and U7;
`artifacts/nbflip-steer-back/removed.patch`, `nbAirPotential`), an earlier
agent added a "solenoidal air extension". It ran 32 Jacobi sweeps of a
potential over every fine air cell, every step: 34 extra dispatches, with
no convergence test and zero potential only at the outer edge of the fine
support. Its potential never corrected the velocity field. It fed only U6,
the RT0/Pollock flux trace that replaced RK3: U6 read the potential's
gradient into its per-face fluxes (`nbFluxFaces`). The steer-back removed
it because no paper had it (Sato's step 8 is the standard Bridson
extrapolation) and because it could not be measured alone: it only fed U6.
U6 re-added alone helped letters by 1.6 points but raised the seep's
receiving ratio to 1.27. U7's own share of the letters loss (−14% to −27%
over that period) was never separated.

**How Sussman's differs:**
- **Cited.** Sussman 2003, as PARIS states it (Aniszewski et al. 2021,
  "Ensuring volume conservation", Eqs. 65–66 and Fig. 11): after the
  extension, solve ∇h·∇h P̂ = ∇h·ũ for a "phantom" pressure in the first
  two layers of gas cells, then set ũ ← ũ − ∇h P̂ on the extrapolated faces
  only. This makes the velocity that advects the interface divergence-free
  there, "to ensure that the advection of C is conservative".
- **Only the band.** The unknowns are the first two gas layers (L1: an air
  h cell with a liquid face neighbour; L2: an air h cell beside L1).
  Pressure is zero beyond them (Dirichlet). U7 covered every fine air cell.
- **Liquid faces stay fixed.** A face with a liquid cell on either side
  (the projection's faces, the extension's sources) and every wall face
  are Neumann: their values never change. Only faces with air on both
  sides and a band cell on one side change.
- **It corrects the field every reader samples.** The corrected faces are
  written into the extended velocity before the 4h cache and the hanging
  taps, so the trace, the samples' velocity, φ advection and momentum all
  read the same divergence-free band. U7 changed nothing but the flux
  trace's inputs.
- **Solved, not swept.** It is a symmetric positive (semi-)definite system
  with diagonal D. A fixed number of Chebyshev-accelerated Jacobi steps
  (Golub & Varga 1961; Saad 2003, Algorithm 12.1) is a standard linear
  solver with no reductions. Its bounds are those of D⁻¹A, [λmin, 2]: a
  flat two-layer band has λmin ≈ 0.07 (κ ≈ 28). An unbounded gap mode (λ
  near 0) is left as it was, never amplified.
- **Once a frame, after the extension.** The existing multigrid is not
  reused. It is built for the liquid's mixed h/4h correction hierarchy, its
  rows come from the pressure authority, and it is restricted to 4h and
  coarser. A two-cell-thick band has no coarse levels to restrict to.

The cost is reported with the timing gate.

Correction to "it corrects the field every reader samples": the FLIP
delta (`flipDelta` "swept") reads the extension's own slots, which the band
projection did not touch. Every other reader sampled the corrected field.

#### Free flight for detached 4h mass, (a) (2026-10-30)

**The stall.** Under {start: "4h"}, pool-rest's drop (r 0.2 m) held a max
speed of exactly g dt, 0.163 m/s, from step 5 on. The cause:

- NB-FLIP's surface pass (umSurfaceOwner) set phase from the centre phi
  alone, and excluded detached-mass phase by design. That exclusion assumed
  every free surface is particle-backed at h.
- A 4h owner's centre phi is the mean of its 8 corners. A sphere centred on
  a vertex leaves it positive for R < 4.49 cells (0.224 m).
- With no liquid phase there is no extension source, so the extended
  velocity was 0. Forces added g dt by occupancy, and no row projected it:
  every step ended at g dt.
- Region heat is ruled out: the stall is the same with coldFrameSkip off,
  coldRegions fine and regionTiering motion.

**The fix (ruled (a)).** The surface pass writes phase = liquid ||
umDetachedMass(o), the rule the VOF build already had. Inviscid and without
surface tension, a detached drop in free fall is at zero pressure
throughout, so free flight is exact. Scope, as the VOF rule has it: V above
dust, touching no liquid row, and not resting on solid or BULK16. "Resting
on" is the face neighbour along gravity's dominant axis
(uniformMixedGravitySupport, written per advance into the authority and
projection parameters); with zero gravity there is no support test. The
first version, with no support test, ran a 0.08 m film on the floor up to
9.9 m/s.
