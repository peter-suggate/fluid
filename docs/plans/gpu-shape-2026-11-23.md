# GPU-shape scheduling for NB-FLIP on the M1 Max (2026-11-23)

## The shift

Today each stage is one kernel over its own live list, as wide as that list. Dawn serializes every dispatch,
so a dispatch costs max(its dependent-load chain, work / throughput). Most of the ~250–300 dispatches a step are
chain-bound: the machine is 75–90% empty while they run (measured occupancy 9–17%).

New model: the GPU is a fixed **slab** per pipeline, `R_k = 32 cores × resident threads per core for kernel k`
(≤ 98k lanes; heavy brick kernels at register caps 384–832 give ~12–27k). The step is planned as **slab launches**:
integer numbers of slabs filled with independent useful work. Evidence: ride-along (fluid-scratch/perf/ride-along.md)
hid 53% of the fine engine's latency-bound families inside the parent's dispatches at no parent cost.

## Three regimes per dispatch (fill = lanes ÷ R_k)

| Fill | Binding | Lever |
|---|---|---|
| < 1 | chain latency, empty slab | pack independent work into the launch; more lanes or loads in flight per item |
| 1..few, fractional | last-wave tail | persistent grid of exactly n·R_k, strided |
| ≫ 1 | registers, instructions | raise R_k, cut per-element work; never packed |

The real target is memory-level parallelism: outstanding loads ≈ bandwidth × latency (estimate: a few hundred KB,
about one load per lane across a full slab). M0 calibrates this.

## Sources of independent work in a step

1. Dependency-DAG antichains of one step (e.g. lAdvect ∥ uAdvect).
2. Tiers: F and C variants of a family (pmgDown1 ∥ pmgDown1C, projectF ∥ projectC).
3. Engines: parent ∥ fine detail (ride-along generalised).
4. Multigrid: additive (BPX-style) all-level smoothing instead of the sequential V-cycle (21 launches/iter).
5. Across steps: next-step housekeeping (census, residency, alloc) packed into this step, one-step lag.

## Known constraints (measured)

- A packed kernel's register allocation is the max over its bodies (ride-along: parent heavy kernels +4–23%).
  Pack within a register class only.
- Any `var<workgroup>` taxes the whole kernel (lAdvect +45%). Pack within a workgroup-memory class.
- 10 storage buffers a stage: packing across engines/stages needs one arena binding layout for the step.
- Layout constants are WGSL text: packed bodies duplicate specialised code, or pay for a level table.
- Launch count is not the cost (≈5 µs a boundary, ≈1 ns an empty small workgroup). Price by kernel time.

## Plan

- **M0 (measure).** (a) GPU-shape microbench: time vs workgroups × lanes × chain depth × register cap; the knee =
  slab per class; optional native-Metal serial vs concurrent dispatch ceiling. (b) Frame fill census on gate scenes:
  every placement's workgroups, lanes, thread cap, workgroup memory, isolated frozen-state time → fill and the prize
  (time in fill < 1 dispatches minus their throughput cost). Go/no-go: prize < ~25% of the step → stop.
- **M1 (ceiling, frozen state).** Generalise union.mts: same-engine antichains; parent+fine; one additive-MG
  iteration vs the V-cycle. Fixed-state equivalence. Go if ≥ X ms on every scene.
- **M2.** One arena binding layout (≤ 9 storage buffers for every step kernel; engines and tiers as lattice levels).
- **M3.** Slab planner: R_k table, per-scene peak counts, packing classes, generated packed modules, direct
  n·R_k dispatch, GPU-side split from count words (no args kernels).
- **M4.** Algorithm reshapes: additive-MG pressure, rolling housekeeping, lanes per item retuned for small F.

Gates per landing: whole-frame A/B on seep/dam/letters/paddleboats (+ teaser/cm12 for detail) with drift and step time;
fixed-state equivalence per packed launch; unpacked kernels' text identical.

## M0 verdict (2026-11-23)

- **Packing sub-slab work: NO-GO on the parent engine.** Placements below one slab hold 2–10% of the step
  (seep 10, dam 6, letters 3, paddleboats 3, teaser 2); all placements under 50 µs together hold 6–22%.
  63–96% of every parent step is in heavy many-slab kernels (caps 384–832). **Fine engine: GO-candidate**
  (24% sub-slab, 52% in the fill-1–4 tail), consistent with ride-along (~1 ms).
- **The slab is set by the memory system, not residency.** Random gathers saturate at ~8.5k lanes × loads in
  flight (≈4k misses machine-wide, 14.5 G lane-loads/s at 8 MB), independent of L, registers and workgroup memory.
  Only coalesced access has the ~100k-lane residency slab. Random throughput falls **20× from 32 MB to 256 MB**
  (latency only 1.7×): the footprint a kernel gathers from is the first-order cost.
- **Dispatch boundary 2.3–2.7 µs** (Metal serial drain; native serial 2.8 µs). Dawn encodes MTLDispatchTypeSerial
  (verified by disassembly); packing in WGSL matches a concurrent encoder within ~10%.
- **Clock range 3.3–3.7×** (DVFS); streams of small dispatches don't hold the top clock. Real-step clock state unknown.

## M0c (next): is the step bound by gather footprint and clock?

- A: finer footprint ladder (32–1024 MB) and windowed-random (random within moving W-sized windows) to separate
  SLC capacity from translation reach; a clock probe the census can embed.
- B: clock probe inside the real step; for the heavy kernels, footprint gathered, achieved loads/s (counters),
  classification (SLC gather / DRAM-translation gather / coalesced / ALU); slot-order locality of 27-neighbourhoods.
- If heavy kernels sit on the collapsed side of the footprint curve, the "GPU shape" lever is **working-set shape**:
  spatially ordered slots, hot-channel compaction, per-step working sets sized to the SLC.
